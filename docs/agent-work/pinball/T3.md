   distinct colours) cycling through winning lines, symbol pulse/glow, count-up label,
   BIG WIN zoom (≥ 50 × bet credits), coin fountain (CPUParticles2D with coins.png),
   book-flip reveal of the special symbol, expanding-symbol animation across a reel,
